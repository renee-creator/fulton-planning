## Her answers

- She posts every scheduled slot herself. A class team's request, or one the office prepares, waits for her.
- The point person on a slot is a person's name, chosen from the educators list or typed for someone not on it.
- Families see the full steps of a job after they sign up. A switch on the library lets her show them before.
- A specialty or passion visit can link to a thread or project. The link is never required.
- The lost and found and the spare clothes bin are their real names.
- Every card line that said staff now says your point person, 41 lines in all. The cards loaded word for word first, so the earlier wording stays in each card's history.
- Newly planted beds, trees and the hedgerow are still watered in by hand. The cards already say so.
- The fence line compost band is about 18 inches wide and 2 to 3 inches deep.
- The two playdough cards show her a note until a playdough recipe is in the recipe library.
- The word class is written with a lowercase c.
- The director and the office see and edit the library. Future Ideas is the director's alone.
- Each card's job type starts from its group. Quick Jobs at Pickup is anytime. At Home is at home. During the School Day and Classes and Studio are school day. The other groups are scheduled. She can change any card.
- Each card's last line follows the words Done when, with the card's own words unchanged.

## Where the cards live

once/volunteer-jobs.json is her file exactly as she gave it. The one-time step volunteerJobs copies it into the records once, under staff/volunteer, and never writes over cards already there. From then on the records hold the cards, with every edit and its history.
